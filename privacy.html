Who we are

Kopu Games is an independent game studio. This policy covers our mobile games, currently:

  • Peel Away: Pin Sort Puzzle (com.kopugames.peelaway)
  • Slide Out - Arrow Slide Puzzle (com.kopugames.slideout)
  • TiltSort - Gravity Match (com.kopugames.tiltsort)
  • Math Maniac (com.kopugames.mathmaniac)

Contact: support@kopugames.com

The short version

  • Our games have no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. We never ask for your name, email or contact details.
  • Your progress, scores and coins are stored only on your device.
  • Our games show ads through Google AdMob. The ad SDK collects device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, as described below.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states), a consent screen appears before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy button: in Peel Away, Slide Out and TiltSort it is in the in-game shop, in Math Maniac on the Settings screen.
  • Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13.

Data the games store on your device

Level progress, stars, scores, streaks, coins, booster stock, purchase entitlements such as "Remove Ads", and your settings. This data never leaves your device unless your phone's own backup service (for example Google's Android backup) copies app data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Data collected by Google AdMob

Our games use Google AdMob (Google LLC) to show rewarded videos and interstitial ads. When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, subject to your device settings)Ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention and, with consent, personalised ads
IP address and coarse location derived from itAd delivery, regional legal requirements, fraud prevention
Device information (model, OS version, language, screen size, app version)Ad delivery and compatibility
Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, video completion)Ad measurement and reporting; rewarded-ad completion is what grants your in-game reward
Crash and diagnostic data about the ad SDK itselfReliability of the SDK

Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation for every app in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

Consent and your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: a consent form is shown before any personalised ad. Declining still lets you play; you may see non-personalised or fewer ads.
  • US states with privacy laws: where required, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
  • Change your mind: in Peel Away, Slide Out and TiltSort, open the in-game shop and tap Privacy to reopen the form; in Math Maniac the button is on the Settings screen. It appears only in regions where a form applies. For any game, your device's ad settings above apply too.
  • Remove Ads: where a game sells Remove Ads, buying it stops interstitial ads. Rewarded videos remain available as an optional choice.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We receive a purchase token and product id to unlock what you bought; we never see your payment details. Refunds follow the store's policy.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and security

We do not operate servers that store your personal data. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own policies. On-device data stays until you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, or to object to it. Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests are best addressed through your device settings and the ad partners' tools linked above. For anything else, email support@kopugames.com.

This website

kopugames.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party fonts or scripts. GitHub, as the host, may log technical data such as your IP address for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you pick a light or dark theme, that choice is stored in your own browser only.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and update the effective date above. Material changes will also be mentioned in the game's store listing notes.
