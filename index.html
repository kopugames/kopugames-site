How we make games

Fair by design

The rules we hold every game to, from the first greybox prototype to the store release.

Learn it in ten seconds

One mechanic per game, taught by playing rather than by a wall of tutorial text.

Solvable, every time

Puzzle levels are generated, then checked by a solver. If a board cannot be cleared, it never ships.

Short sessions

Most levels take a minute or two. Play one at the bus stop or twenty on the sofa.

Ads you choose

Hints and bonuses come from optional rewarded ads. Everything in the game can be earned by playing.

The studio

One developer, a lot of prototypes

Kopu Games is an independent studio run by a single developer. Every game starts as a greybox prototype and only gets art once the core puzzle is fun on its own.

We make the games we want on our own phones: no dark patterns, no fake countdowns, no paywall in front of the fun. If a level ever feels unfair, we want to hear about it.
