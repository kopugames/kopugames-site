Peel Away

Pin Sort Puzzle

A calm sorting puzzle about layered plates and the pins that hold them. Read the layers, plan the order, and peel the board clean.

Coming soon toGoogle Play Play it early

How to play

Three things to know

  1. Pull a pin

    Tap a pin and it drops into your tray. Three pins of one color fill that color's dock.

  2. Peel the layers

    Pull the pin holding a plate up and the plate peels away, revealing the pins underneath.

  3. Mind the tray

    The tray only holds a few pins. Pull in the wrong order and you run out of space.

Features

Why you will keep playing

  • Every board is checked solvable by a solver before it ships.
  • Short levels that fit a coffee break. No timers, no lives.
  • Stars for first-try clears, so replaying a level always has a point.
  • A flip move for the trickiest boards, hints that show the next real move, and undo.

Support

Questions and answers

Still stuck? Email support@kopugames.com.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your phone. There is no account or sign-in. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data resets your progress; your phone's own backup (for example Android backup) may restore it on a new device.

I am stuck on a level. Is it really solvable?

Yes. Every level is checked by a solver before it ships. Use undo or restart to try another order, or watch an optional ad for a hint that shows the next correct move.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads in the in-game shop. It stops the ads that play between levels. Rewarded videos stay available as an optional way to earn hints and coins.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open the shop and tap Privacy to reopen the consent form (it shows in regions where one applies). You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone's settings.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email support@kopugames.com with the game name, the level or mode, and your phone model. A screenshot helps a lot.
