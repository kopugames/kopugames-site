Math Maniac

Think fast, tap faster

Mental math as an arcade game. A rule appears, like multiples of 4 or the answer to 47 + 28, and the numbers start moving. Tap the right ones, skip the rest, and keep your combo alive.

How to play

Three things to know

  1. Read the rule

    Every round starts with one rule: multiples of 2, odd numbers, the answer to a sum.

  2. Tap fast

    Tap the numbers that match before they reach the bottom. Right taps build your combo; wrong taps cost you.

  3. Learn as you go

    A wrong tap shows a one-line explanation, and the campaign raises the difficulty as you improve.

Features

Six ways to play

  • Grid Rush: clear every match in a 4x4 grid before the timer runs out.
  • Ladder: tap the matching numbers in ascending order.
  • Odd One Out: four numbers fit the rule. Find the one that does not.
  • Equation: solve the sum and pick the answer from six.
  • Boss: correct taps damage the boss, and misses heal it.
  • Daily workout: six short rounds that adapt to your accuracy and keep a streak going.

Support

Questions and answers

Still stuck? Email support@kopugames.com.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your phone. There is no account or sign-in. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data resets your progress; your phone's own backup (for example Android backup) may restore it on a new device.

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep the game free. Math Maniac shows ads through Unity Ads; rewarded videos are optional. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone's settings.

Is it good for practising mental math?

That is the idea. The daily workout picks the difficulty of each round from your own accuracy, and every wrong tap explains the right answer in one line.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email support@kopugames.com with the game name, the level or mode, and your phone model. A screenshot helps a lot.
